# Art Direction Brief — Sejarah Indonesia Documentary

## Technical Frame
- **Target Medium:** HyperFrames 1920×1080 (16:9 widescreen) at 30 fps
- **Asset Role:** Full-bleed scenic background plates (`scene-01` through `scene-08`) animated with slow Ken Burns camera motion
- **Dimensions & Format:** `1920×1080` RGB PNG (normalized with Pillow `LANCZOS`)
- **Safe Composition:** Visual focal point positioned in the upper/right 65% of the frame, leaving calm atmospheric negative space in the lower-left and bottom third for editorial cinema typography.

## Visual System
- **Art Style:** Hand-painted archival gouache and Indonesian *Mooi Indië* museum documentary illustration — visible natural brushwork, warm matte paper/canvas grain, dramatic golden-hour or twilight chiaroscuro lighting, rich atmospheric perspective.
- **Palette Roles:**
  - Volcanic Ochre / Gold Accent: `#C88A3B`
  - Deep Twilight Indigo (shadow/scrim base): `#161D2B`
  - Temple Stone / Neutral Midtone: `#7A6E5D`
  - Tropical Emerald: `#2B5B44`
  - Parchment Cream (highlights/text): `#F4EFE6`
  - Merah Putih Crimson: `#B81D24`
- **Strict Exclusions:** Zero text, letters, numbers, maps with labels, logos, UI frames, borders, watermarks, or glossy 3D/cyberpunk AI look.
